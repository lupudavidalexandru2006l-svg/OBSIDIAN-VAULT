# Hit Games 2026-10

What is actually winning right now on Roblox and among small, cheap Steam games, and what a kid-safe (ages 5-18, mostly mobile) Roblox game can borrow from each. Numbers are only given where a source stated them; trackers disagree a lot, so treat every number as approx. Snapshot dates matter: Roblox live counts swing 5-10x in weeks.

> [!summary] Short version
> Roblox hits right now are **simple "get a thing → it earns for you → upgrade → get a rarer thing" loops** with a social twist (stealing, trading, global shops) and **weekly timed events**. Steam viral hits are **cheap, chaotic co-op games** whose funny moments make **10-second clips**. Both reward *showing up* and *showing off*.

---

## Roblox: top and rising games (2025-2026)

### Steal An Egg (and Collect Rare Pets)
- **What:** Pet-hatching incremental game where you grab eggs from biomes (or other players' bases), hatch them and let pets earn money. Launched July 25, 2026.
- **Numbers:** approx. 1.6M concurrent on Sept 30, 2026 (GGAID via StudioKrew); record peak approx. 14.2M (Sept 19, 2026); approx. 6.1B visits. Still #1 in mid-October (RoWatcher: approx. 953K-986K).
- **Core loop:** Grab eggs → hatch → pets earn cash → upgrade treadmill/base for Speed → reach the next biome's rarer eggs.
- **Hook:** Stealing from other players adds drama; biomes rotate nightly; rift bosses every 30 min; a Saturday "Admin Abuse" event and weekly drops; a crossover with the Steal a Brainrot creator made its record peak.
- **UI/HUD:** Typical simulator HUD: cash/speed counters top, a few big side buttons (pets, shop, rebirth), world signs for zones.
- **Fit for kids:** Borrow the **egg → pet → income → speed → new zone** chain, nightly rotating zones and a fixed weekly event day. Do **not** copy real loss from theft. Adapt: "borrow" or "tag" an egg that makes a *copy* or a bonus for the grabber while the owner loses nothing, or a team "egg relay" game.

### Grow a Garden (and Grow a Garden 2)
- **What:** Calm farming game made by a teen in a few days; set a Roblox record of approx. 21.6M concurrent in June 2025 (AP). Grow a Garden 2 was listed approx. 518K on RoWatcher's emerging chart (undated), but had dropped out of the top 50 by September.
- **Core loop:** Buy seeds → plant → wait/grow → harvest and sell → buy rarer seeds.
- **Hook:** A **global seed shop that restocks every 5 minutes**, the same for everyone, so players tell each other when rare seeds drop. Veterans gift seeds to newbies. Low stress, no combat.
- **UI/HUD:** Minimal: money, a few shop buttons, a restock timer.
- **Fit for kids:** Excellent model. Borrow the shared restock moment (talk-about-it event), gifting and calm pace. Watch out: the 5-min timer can become FOMO pressure for little kids; keep it rewarding but never punishing to miss.

### Steal a Brainrot
- **What:** Capture-the-flag-style base game with meme characters ("brainrots"); creator known as Sammy (BRAZILIAN SPYDER). Reported peak of approx. 25M concurrent (secondary reports, 2025). Approx. 101K-108K concurrent in late Sept 2026; was #1 by visits the quarter before Steal An Egg.
- **Core loop:** Buy/steal brainrots → they earn cash in your base → lock your base → rebirth for bonuses.
- **Hook:** Absurd meme humor, outplaying/annoying friends, a timed **Lock Base** button (about 30-60 s depending on source and rebirths).
- **Fit for kids:** The meme-collection and silly-character angle works for 7-15. The core "steal and screw over other players" fun (Kotaku's phrase) creates real loss and frustration for young kids; adapt into harmless swaps or mini-game duels. Pay-for-longer-lock (VIP adds lock time) is pay-to-protect; avoid.

### 99 Nights in the Forest
- **What:** Co-op survival with a campfire and lost children to rescue; approx. 14.2M all-time peak, approx. 26-30B visits; 20th Century Studios bought film rights.
- **Core loop:** Gather wood and food by day → keep the fire lit → survive the night → push further into the forest.
- **Hook:** A clear finish line (99 nights), co-op roles, bi-weekly updates plus a weekly "Update Party".
- **Fit for kids:** Borrow the **countable goal** ("night 37 of 99") and co-op campfire. Must drop the horror (creature hunting you, dark woods). Adapt: "99 Days on Sunny Island", keep the lamp lit, friendly lost animals to rescue.

### Ride A Pet
- **What:** Find eggs, hatch, and ride your pets; speed is both progression and transport. Approx. 208K concurrent on Sept 30, 2026; approx. 1.4M-1.6M peak; 6-player servers. Dev: "this game is gud".
- **Core loop:** Ride → find eggs → hatch faster mount → reach rarer eggs.
- **Hook:** The reward *is* the vehicle: you feel every upgrade. Weekly updates (weather mutations, Volcano Island).
- **Fit for kids:** Ideal. Fully kid-safe; small servers feel friendly. Clones in October: Ride a Dino (approx. 39K), Bike For Egg! (approx. 60K).

### Brookhaven RP
- **What:** Open roleplay town, most-visited Roblox experience (approx. 88B visits); approx. 330K concurrent in Sept 2026.
- **Core loop:** No goal; pick a house, job, car, and act out stories with others.
- **Hook:** Pure social play; Friday updates (cooking, vehicles, Jurassic event).
- **Fit for kids:** Borrow a small roleplay hub (a town square or home base) inside our game. Roleplay spaces need strong moderation design (no private free-text systems, Roblox filtering only).

### Adopt Me!
- **What:** Pet-raising and trading; approx. 135K concurrent (Sept 2026), approx. 45B visits.
- **Core loop:** Do pet chores (feed, sleep) → pet grows → 4 grown = Neon → 4 Neons = Mega Neon.
- **Hook:** Daily chores toward a long goal; every pet is a trade asset.
- **Fit for kids:** Borrow **merge-4-to-upgrade** and caring chores. Trading is a scam magnet for young kids; if we ever add trading, use fixed-value swaps or "gift only" with a confirm screen.

### +1 Speed Keyboard Escape (and the "+1 Speed" genre)
- **What:** Every step makes you faster; run a candy/chocolate escape course. Approx. 307K live (June 2026), approx. 138K (Sept 2026).
- **Core loop:** Walk → speed number goes up → go further → rebirth.
- **Hook:** A number that **only goes up, every second**. Very easy to understand.
- **Fit for kids:** Great for 5-8. Genre is crowded and clones fade fast (+1 Speed Monkey Escape fell from approx. 248K to under 21K in a month). Use it as a layer, not a whole game.

### Murder Mystery 2
- **What:** Short social deduction rounds (murderer, sheriff, innocents); passed 1M concurrent in summer 2026.
- **Hook:** Quick rounds, cosmetic knife/gun trading, seasonal events.
- **Fit for kids:** Borrow **short rounds with secret roles** (hide-and-seek, "who has the golden egg?"). Do not copy weapons or killing.

### Anime Dice
- **What:** Luck-based idle; roll dice for units, place them on your plot, upgrade dice. Released Aug 15, 2026; approx. 76K concurrent, 99% rating; updates every Saturday.
- **Hook:** Rolling is exciting; a strict Saturday update rhythm.
- **Fit for kids:** The roll animation is fun, but random-roll-for-units sits near gambling. If used: earned rolls only, never sell rolls for Robux, show odds, pity counter.

### Fishing sims: Fish It! and Fisch
- **What:** Cast, reel, sell fish, upgrade rods, unlock spots. Fish It! approx. 75K-143K and Fisch approx. 74K concurrent in 2026; Fisch runs seasonal events (FischFright).
- **Fit for kids:** Calm, collectible, skill-light. A fishing mini-game is a safe add-on for almost any theme.

### Dress To Impress
- **What:** Fashion round: dress to a theme, others vote. Approx. 79K (June 2026), approx. 10B visits.
- **Hook:** Creative expression + voting; appeals strongly to girls 8-14.
- **Fit for kids:** Borrow themed build/dress rounds with voting by stars (no free-text).

### Blox Fruits
- **What:** Long anime action RPG; eat fruits for powers, level up across seas. Approx. 245K concurrent (Sept 2026), approx. 65B visits.
- **Hook:** Tradable fruit economy, very long progression.
- **Fit for kids:** Too grindy and combat-heavy as a model for us; the "eat a fruit, get a power" fantasy is borrowable as a cosmetic power-up.

### What's rising in October 2026 (RoWatcher)
- Week of Oct 12-18: **Cat Battles** (approx. 46K, +584%), **Tap Simulator REBORN** (approx. 30K, +627%), **Facecard: Makeup Showdown** (approx. 26K), new: **Bike For Egg!** (approx. 60K), **Ride a Dino** (approx. 39K), **Fake an Egg**, **Lucky Scoop Order**.
- Emerging chart is dominated by egg variants (Break and Steal an Egg, Climb For Animals Eggs, Bike For Egg), "feed / throw food for animals" and Halloween-tagged titles. The current meta is **"[verb] + for/an + Egg"**: one physical action + egg hatching.
- Big declines (e.g. Animal Hospital approx. 358K → 35K in a month, Grow a Garden 2 out of the top 50) show hits fade fast without a steady update schedule.

## Steam: small games that went viral (2024-2026)

### Meccha Chameleon (June 2026)
- **What:** Hide-and-seek where you recolor yourself to blend into the level; up to 10 players. Two Japanese devs, made in about 2 months; approx. $5-6.
- **Numbers:** Approx. 15M copies in about 25 days; peak approx. 340K concurrent (June 21, 2026, SteamDB via Tech Insider).
- **Core loop:** Hiders paint themselves to match a spot → seekers hunt → round ends → swap.
- **Hook:** Clips of near-misses and "they were right there" moments on Twitch/TikTok. No lore, no battle pass.
- **Fit for kids:** **Best single model for us.** Hide-and-seek is universal for 5-18, non-violent, rounds are short, funny reveals. Roblox version: color-match tool, paint-yourself, mobile-friendly tap-to-sample color.

### How to Fish (Aug 2026)
- **What:** Physics co-op fishing that escalates into absurd ways to make money; Dazed Games, $7.99 ($4.95 launch).
- **Numbers:** 1M sold in 2 days; peak approx. 260K-370K concurrent (sources differ).
- **Core loop:** Catch fish → sell → upgrade → unlock islands; chaos with friends.
- **Hook:** "Friendslop": cheap, chaotic co-op, physics that create funny clips; escalating absurdity.
- **Fit for kids:** Borrow the escalating-silliness fishing loop and physics comedy (seagulls stealing fish). Drop the guns and the gambling-your-catch mechanic.

### Scam With Your Friends (Oct 2026), the "scamming game"
- **What:** Co-op comedy sim at a scam call center; you talk by microphone to AI "victims" and trick them out of savings to meet quotas. Early Access Oct 15/16, 2026; approx. 29K peak on launch day (SteamDB via Denfaminicogamer).
- **Core loop:** Take a call → improvise a lie with AI → hit the daily quota → survive the boss's review; chaos events (viruses, raids).
- **Hook:** Free-form AI voice chat creates unpredictable, quotable moments; co-op blame; satire.
- **Fit for kids:** **Do not borrow the theme.** It frames real-world fraud against vulnerable people as the goal; also open voice-to-AI is not allowed for us. What *is* borrowable: **quota + angry-but-funny boss + escalating workplace chaos** (a silly "pizza shop" or "pet hotel" shift with ridiculous customers).

### Gamble With Your Friends (May 2026)
- **What:** Co-op "casino crawler" with one shared bank account; losses become group debt. Swedish TEAM GWYF.
- **Numbers:** 2M copies by June 4, 2026; approx. 42.8K launch peak.
- **Hook:** Shared stakes create shouting-at-friends moments.
- **Fit for kids:** **Avoid the casino.** The borrowable idea is a **shared team wallet** for a group goal (everyone chips in to unlock a team prize).

### PEAK (June 2025)
- **What:** Co-op climbing game from a game jam (Aggro Crab + Landfall), approx. $8.
- **Numbers:** 1M sold in 6 days, 10M+ by mid-Aug 2025; peak approx. 171K concurrent.
- **Core loop:** Climb a mountain together, manage stamina, help each other up, reach the top.
- **Hook:** Friends hauling each other up, falls that are funny, cheap price.
- **Fit for kids:** Great fit: co-op climbing obby where players can give a hand-up. Clear goal (the top).

### R.E.P.O. (Feb/Mar 2025)
- **What:** Co-op horror-comedy: retrieve valuables with a physics grabber while monsters roam. Early Access.
- **Numbers:** Peak approx. 270K concurrent (Mar 23, 2025); sales estimates vary from 1.5M to approx. 10M.
- **Hook:** Proximity voice + physics = funny clips; memes; follows Lethal Company / Content Warning (Content Warning peaked approx. 204K in 2024).
- **Fit for kids:** Horror out. Borrow the **"carry a fragile thing home without breaking it"** co-op physics task (carry a giant egg / cake to the van).

### RV There Yet? (Oct 2025)
- **What:** Up to 4 friends drive an RV through obstacles (winches, bears, snakes). Approx. $4-5.
- **Numbers:** Approx. 1.3M sold in week one; approx. 100K concurrent peak.
- **Hook:** Cheap co-op road chaos, everyone has a job in one vehicle.
- **Fit for kids:** Very borrowable: a shared vehicle where each player has a role (drive, winch, fix). Drop alcohol/smoking items.

### Chained Together (June 2024)
- **What:** Co-op platformer where players are chained together climbing out of hell.
- **Numbers:** Peak approx. 85K-94K concurrent; huge Twitch launch (approx. 217K peak viewers). Kai Cenat and IShowSpeed did a real-life chained stream.
- **Hook:** Forced teamwork = rage and laughter; perfect for streamers. Faded fast (approx. 1-1.5K now).
- **Fit for kids:** Borrow a **"tied together" obby mode** (rope/bubble link) with gentle failure. Replace the hell theme with sky islands.

### A Game About Digging A Hole (early 2025)
- **What:** One-dev, under-a-month 3D digging game: dig, find ores, upgrade shovel; approx. €5.
- **Numbers:** 220K copies in 5 days; peak approx. 7.5K concurrent; clips totalled approx. 22M views across TikTok/Shorts/Reels.
- **Hook:** The title *is* the pitch; satisfying digging clips.
- **Fit for kids:** Perfect simple loop (dig → find → upgrade → dig deeper). Name the game by its one action.

### Bongo Cat (2025)
- **What:** Free desktop idle toy: a cat taps when you type/click and drops cosmetic hats.
- **Numbers:** Peak approx. 194K concurrent; still 100K+ in 2026.
- **Hook:** Zero-effort, always-on, cute; rare hats tradeable on the Steam market.
- **Fit for kids:** Borrow **cute idle companion + rare cosmetic drops** (a pet that taps along and finds hats). No real-money market.

### Schedule I (Mar 2025)
- **What:** Solo-dev drug-dealing business sim; peak approx. 459K concurrent, the biggest solo-dev launch on Steam.
- **Hook:** Co-op business empire, recipe experimentation, customers who come back.
- **Fit for kids:** **Theme is a hard no.** The loop (mix ingredients → find the best recipe → sell to regulars → expand the shop) is great as a smoothie / potion / ice-cream shop. Roblox "Sell Lemons" and "Sell Fries" already prove it.

### Slay the Spire 2 (Mar 2026), for scale only
- Not small, but shows the 2026 bar: approx. 575K peak. Not a model for us.

## Patterns

1. **One verb + one reward in the name.** "Steal an Egg", "Ride a Pet", "Bike For Egg", "A Game About Digging a Hole". The title explains the game. → *How we'd use it:* name our game after its single action ("Bounce for Eggs", "Paint and Hide").
2. **Collect → it earns for you → upgrade → reach rarer stuff.** Eggs/pets/plants that generate income, which buys speed or tools, which opens a new zone. → Build every game around a 3-step chain a 7-year-old can say out loud; put tuning in `Config`.
3. **The reward is the tool.** Ride A Pet's mount, +1 Speed's speed, a better shovel: you *feel* each upgrade. → Make every upgrade visibly change movement or reach, not just a number.
4. **Shared world moments.** Grow a Garden's global 5-minute restock, rift bosses every 30 min, nightly biome rotation. → One server-wide event on a clock (rare egg rain) that kids call out to each other; never punish missing it.
5. **Fixed weekly update day.** Saturday "Admin Abuse" (Steal An Egg), Saturday updates (Anime Dice), Friday (Brookhaven), weekly "Update Party" (99 Nights). Games without it crash within weeks. → Plan a small weekly drop (one new pet/zone/event) on a fixed day; budget for it before launch.
6. **Short rounds, funny reveals (clip-able).** Meccha Chameleon, MM2, Chained Together, PEAK: 10-second moments worth recording. → Design at least one "you were right there!" moment per round; add a quick replay/emote at round end.
7. **Co-op chaos with roles.** RV There Yet, How to Fish, PEAK: cheap co-op where each friend has a job and failure is funny. → Shared vehicles/tasks with 2-4 roles; failure = comedy + instant retry, not loss.
8. **Light social conflict, not real loss.** Stealing/locking is the most viral Roblox mechanic of 2025-26. → Use playful tag/swap: grabbing gives the grabber a bonus *copy*, owner keeps theirs; shield bubbles are free and earned, never sold.
9. **Merge and rarity tiers.** Adopt Me's 4 → Neon → Mega Neon, mutations, sizes, weather. → Merge-4 upgrades and visual mutations (shiny, giant, rainbow) give teens depth without complex menus.
10. **Clear countable goal.** "99 nights", "reach the peak", "next biome". → Always show one progress number toward a named goal on the HUD.
11. **Minimal HUD, world-first.** Hits show currency + 2-4 big buttons; the world (signs, glowing eggs, zones) teaches. → Top: one currency + goal bar; side: 3 big buttons max (Pets, Shop, Rebirth); everything else appears when relevant.
12. **Cheap / free and instantly understood.** Steam hits sit around $5-8; Roblox hits are understandable from the thumbnail. → Thumbnail + title + first 30 seconds must show the whole loop.

## Avoid

- **Real theft / griefing loss** (Steal a Brainrot style) for young players; anything that makes a kid lose what they earned.
- **Gambling-like mechanics**: casino games, paid rolls/eggs, gambling your catch, real-money markets (Gamble With Your Friends, Bongo Cat market, Anime Dice if monetized). Earned rolls only, odds shown, pity timer.
- **Scams, fraud or crime framed as the goal** (Scam With Your Friends, Schedule I), even as satire.
- **Horror and being hunted** (99 Nights, R.E.P.O.); swap in friendly versions.
- **Weapons and killing** (MM2, RIVALS, How to Fish guns).
- **Pay-to-protect / pay-to-win** (paid longer base lock).
- **FOMO timers that punish** missing a restock or event; reward showing up instead.
- **Open voice or free text to AI/players** outside Roblox's filtered systems.
- **Pure clones**: +1 Speed and "Steal a ___" clones fade within weeks; borrow the pattern, add one fresh twist.
