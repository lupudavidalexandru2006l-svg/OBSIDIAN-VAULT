# Game Page Art (research)

What wins on a Roblox game page (the square icon and the 16:9 thumbnails), and what Roblox allows. Feeds [[Game Page Art]] (the style and pipeline). Related: [[Hit Games 2026-10]], [[UI Rules]].

> [!summary] Short version
> One tiny story in one frame, in a **glossy painted "Roblox render" look** (top games generate this with AI, in the same style as our Chunky Pop icons), on a **near-empty, one-hue background**, with **flat cartoon marks** on top ("YOU!" + arrow, "ZZZ", glow swooshes). No title text. Only show what is really in the game, so the first 3 minutes of play match the picture.

## Official Roblox rules (Creator Hub, checked 2026-10-29)
**Icon** ([experience icons](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/experience-icons))
- Square, at least **512×512**. One icon per locale. Must pass moderation.
- Shown as small as **150×150**: preview it small, details must stay clear.
- Must be unique and **match what players will find in the game**; avoid ambiguous symbols.
- Bright, high-saturation colors suit fantasy/happy themes.

**Thumbnails** ([thumbnails](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails), page updated 2026-10-20)
- **16:9, ideally 1920×1080**; jpg/gif/png/tga/bmp. Non-16:9 is stretched. Up to **10** per game, cycled on the detail page.
- **Home page personalization:** activate **2-5** thumbnails, each **under 3 MB** at 1920×1080. Roblox shows each to user groups and measures *qualified play-through rate* (people who click AND keep playing). Keep several active; add new ones with each big update and hold them until the next.
- **Don't put essential text or things at the bottom**: player count and other metadata can cover it.
- Must be relevant and match the real experience. Forbidden (written for videos, applied by moderation to all media): gameplay/UI/mechanics not in the game, misrepresenting the core theme, visuals enhanced beyond what players see, real-life footage, ads/claims/promos ("50% off").
- Text: in-game UI text is fine; overlay text should be sparing and describe gameplay, never sales claims.
- No off-platform links or handles anywhere (Community Standards).
- Moderation is inconsistent in practice (DevForum: a thumbnail got a warning while the same image passed as an icon), so play safe.

## What wins clicks (third-party, informal data)
- **A click is not enough:** personalization ranks by play-through, so a flashy thumbnail that over-promises loses traffic. The thumbnail should preview the first 3 minutes of play ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/roblox-thumbnail-a-b-testing-what-the-data-actually-shows)). Test for 7+ days, not 2-3.
- **One focal point, close crop**, background about 20% of the frame, high contrast against the subject. Faces with emotion. Warm brights (red/orange/yellow) pop on Roblox's dark homepage. More than ~5 visual elements reads as noise. Text under ~40 px (at 1920 wide) disappears at tile size. Viewers decide in under 200 ms ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-roblox-thumbnail-is-a-conversion-ad-stop-designing-it-like-art)). Adopt Me! is the model for consistent art across icon, thumbnails and game.
- Short hook text, if any, names a mechanic or reward ("NEW PET"), never just repeats the game's name.
- Sequels and new twists tease the one new mechanic in the image.

## Case study: the user's reference thumbnail (egg-steal game, 2026-10)
A Roblox avatar runs off with a glowing galaxy egg while a giant dark dragon sleeps behind.
- **It is almost certainly one AI-generated image** made to look like a Roblox render (the user spotted it: "looks very AI generated, like the UI buttons"). Tells: glossy painted shading on everything, perfectly even glows, painted energy curls fused into the dragon, a "render" cleaner than any Studio capture. So top games ship **AI thumbnails in the same painted style as our Chunky Pop icons**. That is the bar, and the whole scene can be generated, not staged.
- **Roblox look kept on purpose:** classic blocky avatar ("bacon hair", huge toothy grin, running pose), a **flat purple baseplate with the studs grid**, a **purple→blue starry gradient sky**. Almost no scenery. It reads as "a Roblox game" while being painted.
- **A story in one frame:** steal the egg while the dragon sleeps. Stakes + joke read in under a second.
- **Flat 2D marks tell the story:** "YOU!" in white with a black outline + a curved red arrow (puts the viewer in the picture), blue "ZZZ" (asleep), neon swoosh strokes in yellow and magenta round the dragon (power/aura). The marks do the explaining, so no title text.
- **One hue family** (purple/magenta) fills the frame; the yellow strokes, red arrow and glowing egg are the only pops. The egg (the reward) is the brightest thing.
- **Layout:** hero on the left third, big and close, slightly cut by the frame edge; the creature fills the right diagonal. White sticker outline round the hero. Nothing important in the bottom strip.
- **Takeaway:** generate the full scene with AI in this glossy Roblox-render look, but only show things that are really in the game (its creatures, items, places), so it stays honest. Add word marks afterwards in a real font: AI lettering is unreliable.

## Patterns to reuse
1. **Mini-story with a twist** (sneak past a sleeping giant, the kid scares the monster): one verb, one reward, one obstacle.
2. **"YOU" marker** on the player avatar: viewer self-insertion.
3. **The reward glows and is the brightest object.**
4. **Scale contrast:** small hero, giant creature or giant reward.
5. **Empty one-colour stage** (baseplate + gradient sky) behind the subjects.
6. **Flat doodle layer** (arrows, ZZZ, motion lines, swooshes, sparkles), max 3 marks.
7. **Classic Roblox avatars** with exaggerated happy faces read as "this is Roblox, this is you".

## Avoid
- Characters, creatures or places that aren't in the game (misleading; moderation risk; play-through drops).
- Sales claims, "FREE", "50% OFF", off-platform handles or links.
- Text in the bottom ~15%; title text that just repeats the game name.
- Scary faces, threatening monsters, dark horror grading (our kid rules). A sleeping or goofy giant is fine.
- More than ~5 elements.
